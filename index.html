UHMWPE Profile & Chainguide

Trolly Wheels / Castor Wheels

Textile Solution Bangladesh supplies premium imported Heavy Duty Trolley Wheels and Castor Wheels for industrial material handling applications. Our wheels are designed for heavy load operation, smooth movement, low maintenance, and long service life in demanding industrial environments.

Fusing Machine Belt

A fusing machine belt is a high-performance conveyor belt used in garment fusing machines to bond fabric layers using heat and pressure. These belts are typically made from PTFE (Teflon) coated fiberglass fabric, designed to withstand high temperatures and continuous operation.

Transmission Flat Conveyor Belt

Textile Solution Bangladesh supplies premium-quality Open Endless Nylon Polyester Sandwich Power Transmission Flat Conveyor Belts for high-speed power transmission and industrial conveying applications in Bangladesh.
